💡 Speaking tips

  • Speak in full sentences, not single words — “I live in Dhaka,” not just “Dhaka.”
  • Don't worry about being perfect. Fluency comes from practice, not from fear.
  • Practice 10 minutes every day — it beats 2 hours once a week.
  • After a correction, say the right sentence 3 times out loud.
