Interview day tips

FROM REAL INTERVIEWS

Officers decide quickly. These simple habits make a big difference in every country.

Do this

  • Answer the question that was asked — short, clear, and direct.
  • Know your own documents: course, dates, costs, and sponsor.
  • Speak confidently and maintain friendly eye contact.
  • Show strong ties to your home country: family, job, property.
  • Dress neatly, arrive early, and carry organised documents.

Avoid this

  • Never give false information or fake documents.
  • Do not memorise long speeches — officers notice immediately.
  • Do not say you want to settle permanently on a temporary visa.
  • Do not argue, interrupt, or answer questions that were not asked.
  • Do not give one-word answers — add one supporting detail.
